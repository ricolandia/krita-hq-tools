HQ Tools

Comic tools for Krita: screentones and hatching, vector balloons and sound effects, an asset library, a 3D viewer, a perspective library, palettes, a page manager, brushes with shortcuts and a hub to open and close the modules.

Installation

In Krita: Tools > Scripts > Import Python plugin and pick the ZIP file. Then enable the plugin in Configure Krita > Python Plugin Manager and restart Krita.

If you installed from the ZIP, also copy hq_tools.action (included in the ZIP) into ~/.local/share/krita/actions/ so the default brush shortcuts show up in Configure Krita > Shortcuts. In development mode, scripts/install-dev.sh does that automatically.

The dockers show up in Settings > Dockers with the "HQ Tools" prefix. To use them as tabs in a single panel, drag one docker over another: Krita groups them automatically and you can ungroup by dragging back.

Screentones and hatching

Applies the native Screentone generator as a (non-destructive) fill layer inside the active group, optionally limited by the selection. The frequency is given in lines per inch (LPI) and the plugin computes the cell size from the document DPI; the X/Y position field shifts the screen without moving the layer. "Edit selected" loads the options of the selected screentone or mask; applying again updates the same layer/mask in place. The "Empty mask (reveal by painting)" mode creates the tone invisible and painting on the mask reveals it. "Show area" turns the mask into a selection. "Reuse identical screentone" avoids duplicating the same tone (a different position counts as a different tone). The "Install patterns and textures (kit)" button copies 11 own patterns (papers, screentones, manga screentone, hatching, grain) into Krita; they show up in "tone with pattern" mode.

Halftone applies the Halftone filter as a mask on the active layer, converting painted tone into dots or lines; on a CMYK document, the "Per-channel colors" mode uses the printing angles (15/75/0/45). For cross-hatch, apply preset A and then B at complementary angles.

The "Effect lines" tab inserts speed (focus) or parallel lines as a vector layer. With an active selection, the lines use its area (parallel) or its center (focus) instead of the percentage fields.

Balloons

Shows the SVG models from a folder (by default, ~/.local/share/krita/hq_tools/balloons, with samples and public domain balloons on first run). Double-click to insert the balloon into the active group as a vector layer. With an active selection, the balloon lands shrunk (never enlarged) and centered in it, and the selection is cleared; without a selection, it lands at the center of the current view instead of the document corner. You can draw your own balloons and save them into that folder. The "Krita symbols" button opens Krita's native docker for the installed libraries, and "Install comic fonts" installs the included fonts (12 OFL families: Bangers, Comic Relief, Patrick Hand, Comic Neue, Londrina Solid/Shadow/Outline/Sketch, Nanum Pen Script, Gaegu and Boogaloo, with credits in CREDITS.en.md).

Sound effects

Catalog of sound effects (8 samples) in ~/.local/share/krita/hq_tools/onomatopeias. Double-click to insert as a vector layer into the active group (with a selection, it lands shrunk and centered in it, clearing the selection; without one, at the center of the view); draw your own models in Inkscape and save them as SVG in the folder.

Project library

Create your own balloons, panels and sound effects inside Krita: pick the layer type (Vector or Paint) and "Create new resource" opens a 15 x 15 cm square document at 300 dpi. Vector: draw with shapes and text and save as SVG. Paint: draw with a brush and save as a transparent PNG, trimmed by the active layer. The resource shows up in the list and is inserted into the active group with a double-click (PNG becomes a paint layer, also shrunk and positioned: in the active selection or at the center of the view, clearing the selection). The library folder can be changed; when you create a project through the manager, the library lives in the biblioteca subfolder of the project folder. Rename, Duplicate and Delete act on the selected resource; delete asks for confirmation.

3D viewer

The "HQ Tools: 3D" docker brings a posable low-poly mannequin (author's mesh with the Auto-Rig Pro rig) to use as pose reference. Pick the model (Man or Woman), the body pose (Idle, Fly, Walk, Run, Jump, Boxing, Sitting and Pose A) and the hand pose (Closed, Open, Holding, Thumbs up, Boxing fist and Relaxed, authored for the right hand; each hand has its own pose box and the left one receives the mirrored version). New poses come in as files in poses/corpo/ and poses/maos/. Drag on the preview to orbit; Shift+drag (or the middle button, or the "Move" button) pans the framing; the mouse wheel or the +/− buttons zoom, and "Fit" centers. Click a body part: the sliders for that region appear. "Bend", "Open" and "Twist" follow the rig axes (on this rig: Z, X and Y, respectively); on the arm there is also a slider that bends all fingers at once. The controls live in tabs (Pose, Camera and Insert), with the preview always visible; on the Camera tab, the "Camera:" selector switches between Orthographic (default) and Perspective, with 14/28/35 mm lenses (the lens keeps the framing and changes only the convergence); the "Color:" selector picks the mannequin tone (beige, blue, ice or graphite). The Style selector switches between Shaded, Silhouette (flat) and Outline (silhouette line only); on the last two the preview gets a paper background so the shape shows up in any theme. The insertion flow starts with a selection: draw a rectangle with the selection tool over the target panel. The preview adopts the selection aspect ratio and shows exactly the crop that will be inserted (camera zoom and pan included). "Float on page" only works with an active selection: the window appears over the selection, draggable and resizable (handle or wheel, keeping the aspect ratio), with opacity and the "Lock" button to draw underneath. "Insert as layer" and "Insert as reference" also only work with a selection: they rasterize at its exact size, place the layer below the active layer (the sketch stays on top) and clear the selection; without a selection, a warning asks you to draw one.

Perspective

Library of perspective grids to insert into a panel: one-point (frontal), two- and three-point, bird's and worm's eye (two levels each) and 4- and 5-point curvilinear. Pick the set, draw a rectangular selection over the panel and insert: the grid lands as a vector layer (each line with two nodes, editable) at the selection size, below the sketch; the "Insert as reference" option keeps the layer locked, and one Ctrl+Z undoes it. "Float on page" shows the grid over the selection, with drag to move and wheel to resize. The colors separate the families: blue = verticals/3rd vanishing point; orange = depth and axes; gray = horizontals and horizon.

Moodboard

Project reference board, light because it stores only links: the images chosen in "Add references..." are resized (max side 1600 px) and saved as JPG in the project's moodboard/ folder (the originals stay untouched), and enter the moodboard.kra board as file layers placed on a grid that grows with the references. "Open moodboard" creates or opens the board; double-click (or "Insert into selection") brings the reference to the selected panel as a locked layer, fitted to the selection, for tracing over. Rename and Delete manage the folder without leaving Krita.

Production

Script checklist: paste the text (the same syntax as the pages, with the optional character on the line and the shot type per panel, e.g. plano p1: wide) and use Ctrl+Enter (or "Build checklist"); the script auto-saves (with a "saved HH:MM" hint). Each page becomes a node with the layout panels and each panel has three states (sketch, art and final): click the State column to cycle, and right-click sets it directly. The panel shot appears in the Shot column and in the generated storyboard (label in the panel corner); the lines and characters are in view. The bar on top shows the progress in three colors and "Open next pending" jumps to the first unfinished page. The Start/Pause button counts production time (total and per page); the weekly panel goal shows what is left and the expected delivery date, and double-clicking a page opens its pagina_NNN.kra. The "Syntax..." button shows the full format; roteiro.txt, producao.json and tempos.json live in the project's producao/ subfolder (created with the project in Pages; without a project open, it saves in the plugin default folder and warns to choose the folder), and "Export .md" saves the checklist with checkboxes, emojis and per-page time.

Palettes

Comic and artistic templates (tones, ink, skin, sky, vegetation, flat colors, Zorn, portrait, landscape, dawn, night, earth, pastel, watercolor, gouache, acrylic, retro, BD, superhero, manga, sepia, values, screentone chart) applied directly to the foreground or background color. Pick a swatch and use "Apply to foreground"/"Apply to background" (double-click applies to the foreground); the color applies to brushes that use the foreground color, presets with their own brush color ignore it. The "Install into Krita" button copies the files into Krita's palettes folder: after a restart they show up in Krita's palettes docker, where clicking a color sets the foreground color. The second HQ Tools tab only shows the installed palettes.

Pages

The manager works with the project folder: "New project..." uses the folder of the current saved page (if the page is not saved, the warning "Save the current page in a folder. That folder becomes the project folder." appears). The plugin creates the CPMT comicConfig.json and the library and export subfolders in the folder. "Open project..." reads a CPMT project and "Folder..." opens any folder with .kra files. "Create next page" opens a dialog with format (A4, A5, A3, strip, American, tankobon, square or free in mm), DPI and the number of strip panels; the page is born with the margin guides (0.5 / 1 / 1.5 cm per side) already applied. "Set page template..." uses the current page or a Krita comic template as the base for the next pages (A3 resizes; strip becomes a horizontal strip); the "Generate HQ Tools standard models" button creates own models on the spot (A4, A3, strips 1-3 and grids 2x2/3x3) with margin guides. References: "Reference layer" marks the selected layer and "Import reference (PNG)" inserts a locked reference into the active group. It shows the internal thumbnails, opens with a double-click and allows reordering by dragging. The option to name the balloon layer text makes CPMT use the content in ACBF/EPUB export. Generated pages come with the "Arte" group (Sketch/Color/Ink) and the "Panel mask": painting is limited to the panels; hide the mask in the layers docker to also paint in the gutters and margins.

Brushes

The sets (Sketching, Inking, Watercolor/Gouache, Acrylic/Oil, Screentones) build cards from the presets already installed in your Krita; click the card to activate and right-click assigns it to one of the eight slots, with shortcuts in Configure Krita > Shortcuts > Scripts > HQ Tools (actions "HQ Tools: brush 1" to "brush 8"). To clear, right-click a slot (clear this one or all) or use the "Clear slots" button. The "Packs" tab brings community brushes with verified licenses (Deevad v8.2, David Revoy, CC-BY 4.0; Krita Watercolor Set, Vasco Basqué, CC-0), installable with one click; each pack has its license and credits in CREDITS.en.md. The "Community" tab lists the presets from those packs after they are installed. After installing a pack, restart Krita. On the "Packs" tab, the "Install bundle..." button copies your own .bundle file into Krita.

Hub

Control center to open and close the modules: one button per docker, checked while the dock is open (click again to close). The buttons are grouped by dividers: Pages/Production, Moodboard/Library, Perspective/3D, Palettes/Brushes, Screentones/Balloons and Sound effects. The "Close the current one when opening another" option keeps only one module open at a time; unchecked, the docks open together.

Configuration

Preferences live in ~/.local/share/krita/hq_tools/config.json and the user screentone presets in ~/.local/share/krita/hq_tools/screentone_presets.json. Each module can be turned off in the modules key of the configuration file (Krita must be restarted). The interface follows the system language (Portuguese locale uses Portuguese, everything else uses English); the environment variable HQ_TOOLS_IDIOMA=pt|en forces a language.
